## Hi, I'm Emma 👋

I'm a programmer with a background in physics and cs, and a foreground in the history of physics and supercomputing in the 20th century. 

While transitioning back from history of science to programming and curious about how LLMs were changing the nature of coding, I made a few tools for exploring the trajectories of literature and researchers that built the present:
- https://emma.jablonski.us/academic-descent/ (https://github.com/emmjab/academic-descent)
- https://emma.jablonski.us/nobel-map/ (https://github.com/emmjab/nobel-prize-map)

More recently I dove into deep learning via [this Practical Deep Learning course](https://course.fast.ai/) with a study group at the [Recurse Center](https://www.recurse.com/)! If you're curious you can read through [my notes](https://emmjab.github.io/).

I attended the [Climate Change AI Virtual Summer School 2026](https://www.climatechange.ai/events/summer_school2026).
